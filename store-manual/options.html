Settings

Your details, and the interface language.

Language

All texts in the extension change when you pick a language.

Your details

These stay in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the extension never invents a value for a field you leave empty.

Saved

What it will not do

  • It never presses a form's own submit button.
  • It never ticks a terms, privacy or consent checkbox for you.
  • It never clicks away cookie or consent banners.
  • It never creates e mail addresses or invents a name, address or date of birth.
  • It sends nothing over the network. There is no server.

Known limits

  • Every site is different, so an unusual layout may be filled only partly. The popup lists the fields it could not fill.
  • CAPTCHAs and proof of work are not solved. Nothing here bypasses them.
  • Sites that require a one time code by e mail need you to paste the code in yourself.
  • Filled fields are marked in green so you can see what was typed before you submit.
