Fills in web forms from details you saved in Settings, so you do not retype the same thing on every sign up or login page.

**What it does**

- Types your saved name, e mail, password, username, address, city, postal code, country, date of birth and gender into the fields it finds
- Works on modern sites that build their forms from custom elements and shadow DOM
- Handles both a normal country dropdown and the custom listbox that libraries like Radix build
- Fills one time code boxes, from a code you copied out yourself
- Marks every field it filled in green, and lists what it could not fill
- Lists any checkbox the site still wants ticked

**What it will not do**

- It never presses a form's own submit button
- It never ticks a terms, privacy or consent checkbox for you
- It never clicks away cookie or consent banners
- It never creates e mail addresses, and has no e mail provider
- It never invents a name, an address, a date of birth or a password
- It sends nothing over the network. There is no server, and the code has no network calls at all

Accepting a legal agreement and pressing submit on a website are things a person does, so they are things this extension leaves to you.

**Getting started:** open Settings and fill in your details, open the page with the form, click the icon and press Fill the form, then review, tick whatever the site requires, and submit.

**Rate limit:** three automatic fills per hour.

**Languages:** English, Swedish, Turkish, Arabic, Spanish, German, French.

**Privacy:** everything stays in your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.
