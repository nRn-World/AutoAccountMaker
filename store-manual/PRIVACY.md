# AutoAccountMaker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

AutoAccountMaker Form Filler is a browser extension for Chrome and Firefox that helps you fill in web forms from the details you saved. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

**Short version:** the extension has no server of its own, no analytics and no tracking. Your data stays in your browser. It makes zero network calls.

## What the extension stores, and where

| Data | Why | Where it is stored |
|------|-----|--------------------|
| Settings (name, email, etc.) | To fill forms for you | The browser's local extension storage, on your device only |

The details used to fill forms are provided by you in Settings. It does not ask for, read or store your real personal information unless you enter it.

## What leaves your device

The extension makes NO network requests.

- **Websites you fill forms on**: the extension fills in the form on the page you are on, exactly as if you typed it. What that website does with the data is up to that website.

Nothing is sent to the developer. There is no analytics or crash reporting.

## Page access

The extension runs a script on web pages so it can find and fill form fields. It acts only when you press a button in the extension. It does not collect browsing history and does not send page content anywhere other than the form you are filling in.

## Your control

- You can see and delete your saved details in Settings.
- Removing the extension deletes the data stored on your device.
