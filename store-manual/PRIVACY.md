# AutoAccountMaker: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-17

AutoAccountMaker is a Chrome extension that creates test accounts on websites: it makes a temporary e-mail address, fills in a sign-up form with generated details, reads the verification e-mail and logs in. This policy explains what data the extension handles.

**Short version:** the extension has no server of its own, no analytics and no tracking. Your accounts stay in your browser. The only outside service it talks to is the temporary e-mail service it needs to receive verification e-mails.

## What the extension stores, and where

| Data | Why | Where it is stored |
|------|-----|--------------------|
| Accounts the extension created for you (website, temporary e-mail, generated username, generated password, time, verified or not) | So you can log in again later and see your accounts | `chrome.storage.local`, on your device only |
| Settings (interface language, how many accounts to keep, optional RapidAPI key) | So the extension remembers your choices | `chrome.storage.local` |
| Pro license key, if you buy one | To unlock Pro. The key is checked on your device, nothing is sent anywhere | `chrome.storage.local` |
| A timestamp for each automated sign-up | To apply the free-version limit of one sign-up every three hours | `chrome.storage.local` and `chrome.storage.sync` (Chrome may sync these timestamps between your own browsers) |

The names and other details used to fill forms are generated by the extension. It does not ask for, read or store your real personal information.

## What leaves your device

The extension makes network requests only to the services it needs:

- **mail.tm (api.mail.tm)**: a free temporary mailbox service. When you press Register, the extension asks mail.tm to create a mailbox and later asks it for the verification e-mail. mail.tm receives the temporary address and the e-mails sent to it. See the mail.tm privacy policy.
- **temp-mail via RapidAPI (privatix-temp-mail-v1.p.rapidapi.com)**: only if you choose this provider in Settings and enter your own RapidAPI key. The key is sent to RapidAPI with the request.
- **Sponsored link (free version only)**: the free version shows a clearly labelled "Sponsored" card. It is a plain link to an offer from an advertising partner (Adsterra). Nothing is loaded from the partner while the card is shown. The partner's site only opens, in a new tab, if you click the card, and its own privacy policy applies from then on. Pro hides the card.
- **Ko-fi**: the Settings page has a link to the developer's Ko-fi page. It opens only if you click it.
- **Websites you register on**: the extension fills in the sign-up form on the page you are on, exactly as if you typed it. What that website does with the data is up to that website.

Nothing is sent to the developer. There is no analytics or crash reporting, and no advertising other than the one clearly labelled sponsored link described above.

## Page access

The extension runs a script on web pages so it can find and fill form fields and read a verification code on the page. It acts only when you press a button in the extension. It does not collect browsing history and does not send page content anywhere other than the form you are filling in.

## Your control

- You can see, copy, export and delete every saved account under Settings, Account vault.
- Exports to CSV and PDF are not encrypted. The encrypted backup (.enc) needs a password that only you know.
- Removing the extension deletes the data stored on your device. Clearing the vault removes the saved accounts at any time.

## Purchases

Pro licenses are sold through Ko-fi. Payment and your e-mail address are handled by Ko-fi under its own terms. The extension does not see your payment details.

## Children

The extension is not directed at children.
